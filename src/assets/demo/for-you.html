Product overview · for non-scientists

See your genetics clearly

Upload a paper or genetic report. Watch the mutations appear on the gene.

Genetics is often locked inside dense papers and jargon-filled reports. Baja.bio finds the genes and variants for you and shows exactly where they sit — turning unfamiliar codes into a picture you can explore.

A gene drawn as a strip: teal blocks are its working parts, and each pin marks a variant exactly where it sits. This is what Baja.bio builds from a document you upload.

Three words that unlock genetics
GeneA stretch of DNA carrying instructions for one job — like one recipe in a huge cookbook.
ExonA working part of a gene that carries instructions — shown as a teal block above.
Mutation / variantA place where DNA is spelled differently. Its location provides an important clue.
Learning and exploration only. Baja.bio helps you understand and visualize what a document says. It does not diagnose, interpret clinical risk, or replace a physician or genetic counselor. Your files remain yours.
